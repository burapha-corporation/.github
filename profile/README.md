# Burapha Corporation

**บูรพา คอร์ปอเรชั่น**

</div>

Burapha Corporation runs three web platforms and a group of businesses. This page says what each one is; it claims nothing that is not live. The meaning of each brand is set by the BC Design System (the `PLATFORMS` constant), and if this page disagrees with it, the Design System wins.

## Burapha platforms

| Platform | Role | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| [burapha.xyz](https://burapha.xyz) | SMART COMPANY | The corporate site: company profile, the group's businesses, news, careers, contact |
| [burapha.org](https://burapha.org) | SMART CITY | "Smart City for Homes, Projects and Farms": consultancy and builder on Smart City and BCG lines. Carbon Bank and Seed Bank are Proposed |
| [burapha.app](https://burapha.app) | SMART APP | "Smart Application for Data, Homes and Buildings". Burapha Energy Monitor is accepting pilot buildings; Burapha Data and Home IoT are Proposed |

Two more products sit beside them:

- [burapha.space](https://burapha.space): the property platform (also white-labelled as thaiproperty.online)
- [burapha.store](https://burapha.store): the BOQ & quotation app (live since 4 Nov 2026)

## Repositories

The code is in one umbrella repository, [`burapha`](https://github.com/burapha-corporation/burapha) (private): the Elixir/Phoenix API, the sites under `apollo/`, the group's other projects under `artemis/`, the design system (`design/`), the Capistrano deploy (`infra/`) and every document (`docs/`). Several repositories in this organisation are submodules of it.

## ภาษาไทย

Burapha Corporation (บูรพา คอร์ปอเรชั่น) ดำเนินสามเว็บแพลตฟอร์ม ได้แก่ burapha.xyz (SMART COMPANY) burapha.org (SMART CITY) และ burapha.app (SMART APP) พร้อมแพลตฟอร์มอสังหาฯ burapha.space และแอปใบเสนอราคา burapha.store และกลุ่มธุรกิจ Ounrak · Rachan · Suphap · Wanich
